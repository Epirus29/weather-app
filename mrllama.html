Lightning Fury Javazon

Throw javelins that chain lightning across the whole screen, then walk up to bosses and stab them with Charged Strike. One of the fastest farmers in the game and playable on a budget, with one real weakness: monsters immune to lightning.

Skills

SkillPointsWhy
Lightning Fury20Your pack killer
Charged Strike20Your boss killer
Lightning Bolt20Synergy for both
Power Strike20Synergy for both
Pierce1 to 101 is enough with Razortail
Valkyrie, Decoy1 eachTanks that scale with +skills
Dodge, Avoid, Evade1 eachFree damage avoidance
Critical Strike, Penetrate1 eachPrerequisites that help anyway

Order while leveling: Jab, then Power Strike, then Lightning Bolt, Charged Strike at 18, and Lightning Fury the moment you hit 30.

Stats

StatTarget
StrengthEnough for your gear, nothing more
DexterityEnough for max block with your shield
VitalityEverything else
EnergyNone. Mana comes from gear and the merc

Mercenary

Act 2 Nightmare offensive merc for Might. Give him Infinity as soon as you can afford it: it turns most lightning immunes into normal monsters and removes the build's only real problem. Until then, Insight keeps your mana full.

Where to farm

Cows, Chaos Sanctuary, Baal runs, and any Terror Zone without a lightning-immune pack are ideal. Before Infinity, skip areas heavy with lightning immunes or bring a merc that can handle them for you. Charged Strike carries Ubers with the right gear, which is unusual for a farming build.

Cannot be frozen matters more than it looks: a frozen Javazon throws at half speed. Raven Frost solves it.
